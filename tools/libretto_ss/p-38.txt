932. Leaking gas from the ruptured fuel line
catches fire and the truck (chosen at random,
if necessary) explodes. Replace the wrecked
truck marker with a crater marker. Any men
in the truck roll on the Satchel Charge Inside
row as if non-prone. Any men in another
vehicle in the same hex roll on the Satchel
Charge Outside row as if prone. Any men in
the hex and not in a vehicle roll on the
Satchel Charge Outside row according to their
current stance. Any other vehicle in the same
hex rolls on the Satchel Charge Outside row;
if the result is killed or incapacitated, that
vehicle is disabled; otherwise there is no
effect. This can occur more than once per
Mission.
