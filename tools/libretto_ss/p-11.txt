413. [s0] There is a roadblock in I-5. Place
roadblock marker (from Ambush). Gain 1
bonus VP when roadblock is cleared. If in
Operations, at least 1 soldier must be in the
roadblock hex to clear it and two Random

Event Checks must be made. If in Rounds,
roadblock can be cleared by:

1. Throwing satchel charge in the hex.

2. Heavy bazooka penetration (roadblock is
+2 to hit).

3. At least 1 crouching or standing soldier
must be in the roadblock hex and 3 Actions
(which can be shared) must be spent by
soldier(s) in the roadblock hex. Soldiers in the
roadblock hex are considered to be in cover
for purposes of resolving fire combat.

4. Ramming through the roadblock with a
truck. The truck attempting to ram through
the roadblock must be going fast when it
enters the roadblock hex and must have
sufficient MPs to exit the roadblock. The
driver of the truck must conduct 2 Accident
Checks — the first when entering the hex with
the roadblock, and the second to exit the hex.
If either Accident Check is failed, the truck is
automatically disabled in the roadblock hex
and all occupants take damage on the Semi-
Automatic Rifle row (due to being slammed
into the dashboard and windshield by the
sudden stop). If 2 trucks become disabled in
the roadblock hex, the third truck can enter
the adjacent hex and, after a standing or
crouching soldier in either hex spends one
Action to hook up a towline, the driver of the
truck can spend one Action to pull a disabled
truck into the adjacent hex (this is to allow the
third truck to try to ram through the
roadblock without violating the prohibition.
against more than two vehicles in a hex). The
truck attempting to clear the roadblock by
ramming through it must enter and exit the
roadblock hex in the same Turn for the
roadblock to be successfully cleared. The jeep
cannot attempt to clear the roadblock by
ramming through because the small vehicle
lacks sufficient weight to break through the
roadblock.

116. One German soldier chosen at random
goes berserk! For the remainder of the
Mission, or until this German becomes

inactive, conduct the following procedures:
1. The soldier receives 2 Turns per Round
(unless wounded).

2. Each Turn, the soldier stands, conducts one
snap fire at the closest active target, and then
moves toward the closest active target, using
up to half his MPA (rounding down). If there
is no active target in sight, the berserk
German will move toward the nearest active
American and conduct 1 snap fire against the
first target to come into sight if sufficient MPs
remain to do so. The soldier does not fall
prone, even if a free stance change is
available. If the soldier is wounded, he will
stop firing and move only.

3. If the soldier's main weapon jams or he
runs out of ammo, he will use his secondary
weapon, if available. If no weapons are
available, the soldier will use his full MPA
each Turn toward the nearest active US
soldier. The berserk German will not use
grenades.

4, The berserk German’s movement is not
controlled by standard paragraph references.
Instead, move the soldier directly toward the
closest active American, paying normal terrain
costs. Roll one die to resolve movement
choices. The soldier will not enter prohibited
terrain or run through burning buildings,
across destroyed bridges, and so forth (he’s
berserk, not crazy).

5. If the berserk German begins a Turn in a
hex with one or more active Americans, he
will assault to kill the easiest target. If all
Americans in the hex become inactive or exit
the hex, the berserk German will repeat Step
2, above.

6. The berserk German will not panic and is
considered to have a +2 WS when attacking
or defending in assault combat. Berserk
German cannot be given a Turn and does not
make a Grenade Strike PC Check.

Place Berserk marker with 116 on it on the
berserk German as a reminder. The 116 on
the counter is to remind you of this paragraph
for reference. This Event can occur only once
per Mission. Ignore further references to it
after the first German goes berserk.
