598. Remove explosives marker, if there is
one in the hex. Flip bridge counter over to
destroyed side; any vehicles that were on top
of the bridge are disabled (flip their markers
to their destroyed side). Occupants of vehicles
that were on top of the bridge take damage on
Satchel Charge Outside row as if prone;
soldiers who were on top of the bridge but
not in vehicles roll on the Satchel Charge
Outside row in current stance. Soldiers under
the bridge in the supports take damage on
Satchel Charge Outside row as if non-prone;
soldiers beneath the bridge on the ground roll
on the Satchel Charge Outside row in current
stance. The hex is now impassable to vehicles
and costs soldiers 2 MP to enter. Lose 1 VP
when bridge in V-14 is destroyed.
