499. Germans in patrol are not automatically
aware when activated. Each German in the
patrol must conduct a PC Check as follows:
© If in the same hex as an active American,
conduct PC (+2).

* If in a hex adjacent to an active American,
conduct PC (+1).

© If in neither the same nor an adjacent hex,
conduct normal PC.

Any German who fails his PC Check is not
aware in the first Round. All Germans in
patrol are automatically aware in the second
and all subsequent Rounds.

© If there is at least one soldier prone in the
same hex as an active mule, one mule chosen
at random becomes skittish and may step on
the prone soldier(s). All soldiers in the hex
with the skittish mule conduct a PC Check.

active soldier, including those currently under
control. This may occur more than once per
Mission.
