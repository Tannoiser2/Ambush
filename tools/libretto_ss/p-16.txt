237. Barge counters are marked with 237 to
remind you of this paragraph. Barges are
considered large vehicles in the open for fire
combat purposes. A soldier on a barge is
considered to be in cover. Barges do not block
LOS. It costs 2 MP to climb onto a barge
from an adjacent land hex, and it costs 1
Action to climb onto a barge from an all-
water hex. Each barge is worth 3 VP if
destroyed. A barge can be destroyed by:

4. M or H penetration from a bazooka, killing
any soldiers on the barge.

2. Exploding a timed explosive affixed to the
barge (barges are the only target timed
explosives can be used to destroy). Soldier
must be in same hex as the barge to affix a
timed explosive to the barge, but soldier can
be underwater or on the surface to do so. It
costs 1 Action to affix the timed explosive to
the barge and set the fuse. Use a tick, tick
marker as explained in the Mission briefing to
remind you that the timed explosive is set.
Check for dud as for satchel charge. When
the timed explosive goes off, all soldiers on
the barge are killed; all soldiers in the water
in the barge’s hex take damage on the Satchel
Charge Outside row, without first making a
Grenade Strike PC Check; soldiers on the
surface are considered non-prone and soldiers
underwater are considered prone; and the
barge is destroyed (remove barge from the
map).

3. Exploding a satchel charge on the barge
will destroy the barge. There is a —1 modifier
if the satchel charge is thrown from other than
the barge’s hex. Damage is assessed as in Part
2, above.

4. Exploding a grenade on a barge will
destroy the barge if the initial roll on the
Grenade Inside row is incapacitated or killed
(in which case damage is assumed as in Part
2, above). If the initial roll is panic or
wounded, the barge is unharmed, but all
soldiers on the barge take damage on the
Grenade Inside row. Soldiers on a barge do
make a Grenade Strike PC Check prior to any
damage occurring. There is a —1 modifier if
the grenade is thrown from other than the
barge’s hex. If the initial hit roll causes the
grenade to miss the barge, but the roll on the
Grenade Scatter Diagram causes the grenade
to hit the barge, roll 1 die: On an odd result,
the grenade hits the barge; on an even result,
the grenade lands in the water in the barge’s
hex.

Divers can now climb onto the barge, but still
cannot enter a land hex. Make a note of this
paragraph on your Squad Record Sheet.
