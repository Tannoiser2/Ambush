253. An ATG counter has two sides printed
loaded and unloaded to help you keep track of
its status. When an ATG is fired, turn the
counter to its unloaded side; when a crew
member spends an Action to load the ATG,
flip the counter to its loaded side. An ATG
cannot be loaded and fired in the same Turn;
no soldiers other than the original crew can
load or fire the ATG. Americans cannot use a
German ATG for any purpose. This paragraph
number appears on both sides of the ATG
counter in case you want to refer back to this
paragraph during play.

Firing the ATG. The firing soldier's WS does
apply. An ATG can fire only through its
covered arc. If the crew is directed to fire the
ATG and the only target is outside the
covered arc, the crew will spend 2 Actions (1
Action per crew member and both crew
members must have Turns remaining in the
Round to spend the Actions; if one crew
member has 2 Turns and the other crew
member has 1, move each of their IN markers
down one Turn) to change the covered arc to
that the new covered arc is centered on the
target. If only one crew member is active, the
covered arc cannot be changed. If any crew
members have Turns remaining, the ATG can
be fired in the same Turn that the covered arc
is changed, using a —2 modifier for snap fire.
It costs 1 Action to fire an ATG and 1 Action
to load it. An ATG cannot fire and load, or
load and fire, in the same Turn. It costs 1
Action to load an ATG and 1 Action to fire
it.
