remains on the map). All Americans who
failed the PC Check caused by 492 conduct
one snap fire at US 06 (no cost). If US 06 is
KIA by the snap fire(s), lose 4 VP (2 for
KIA, 2 for mistake). If US 06 is incapacitated
by these fires, normal VP loss is incurred. If
US 06 survives the mistaken attacks, he is a
member of your squad for the rest of the

reconduct 492, but Activation Check is now
