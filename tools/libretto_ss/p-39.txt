990. If any truck is currently disabled by
combat or accident, see 932.
* Otherwise, no Event.

991. © If there are any boobytraps on the map
that did not explode when discovered, one
(chosen at random, if necessary) was not fully
defused and now explodes. Any soldiers in the
hex roll on the Grenade Inside row in their
current stance. (If you cannot remember
whether or not a boobytrap exploded, assume
that it did not.) This can occur only once per
Mission; ignore any further reference to this
paragraph once a boobytrap explodes in this
manner.

* Otherwise, no Event.

992. One German weapon fired in the
previous Round is out of ammo. If more than
one German weapon was fired in the last
Round, determine which is out of ammo at
random. Does apply to GE 47, if active. Can
occur more than once per Mission.

993. One non-prone US soldier, determined at
random, occupying a non-road, non-clear hex,
falls. First he must make a PC Check. If

successful, he is uninjured, but falls prone. If

he fails, roll for damage as if he were hit by
Pistol fire. This can occur only once per
Mission. Ignore any further reference to this
paragraph once the first soldier has fallen in
this manner.

994. Go to Condition 3. Mission ends in
Condition 3 when all Americans have exited
the map via the entry hexes (W-1 through
Y-1). You need 20 VPs to win (ignore
Victory Conditions in 269). VP loss for
incapacitated squad members can be avoided
by exiting them.

995. One wounded German soldier,
determined at random, becomes incapacitated
due to blood loss. The Event can occur only
once per Mission. Ignore this paragraph after
the first German soldier is incapacitated in this
manner.

996. One incapacitated German soldier,
determined at random, dies due to his injuries.
This Event can occur only once per Mission.
Ignore this paragraph after the first German
soldier dies in this manner.

997. One wounded US soldier, determined at
random, becomes incapacitated due to blood
loss. This Event can occur only once per
Mission. Ignore this paragraph after the first
US soldier is incapacitated in this manner.

998. One incapacitated US soldier, determined
at random, dies due to his injuries. This Event
can occur only once per Mission. Ignore this
paragraph after the first US soldier dies in this
manner.

999. One active vehicle (chosen at random, if
necessary) blows a tire. Conduct Accident
Check. Vehicle can now be driven only
slowly. If the vehicle has already blown a tire
and you are in Rounds, the vehicle is
immobilized until 5 Actions are spent to put
on the spare tire; Actions can be shared. If in
Operations, there is no effect. This can occur
only once per Mission. Ignore any further
reference to this paragraph after the first tire
blows.
