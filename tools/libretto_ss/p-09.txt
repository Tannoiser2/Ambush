Body: Use Damage Table for attacking weapon type. If penetration

Damage Effects

© No result. Driver: Roll for damage using the Damage Table row
of the attacking weapon. If the driver panics or is wounded, make
an immediate Accident Check. If the driver is incapacitated or killed,
an accident occurs; roll on the Accident Table. Assistant Driver:
Roll for damage using the Damage Table row of the attacking weapon.
Tire: Vehicle cannot be driven fast. If occupied, make an immediate
Accident Check. Tread: If there is light penetration and the vehicle
is occupied, make an immediate Accident Check. If the penetration
is medium or heavy, the half track throws a tread, is immobilized
in its current hex, and cannot move again, Body: If the body is hit
and there is light penetration, one passenger (chosen at random) suffers
damage as determined by rolling on the Semi-Automatic row. If the
penetration is medium, the half track is disabled; passengers must
spend 2 MP to exit the wreck. If the penetration is heavy, the half
track is disabled and all occupants roll on the Semi-Automatic row.
Survivors spend 2 MP to exit the wreck. The half track is considered
a tank for resolving grenade/satchel charge attacks, for conducting
minefield/boobytrap procedures, on the Accident Table, and when
running over soldiers.

Damage Effects

‘Wall: If wall is hit and penetration result is H, the fortified building
is destroyed and all soldiers inside are killed. Replace fortified building
marker with rubble marker; all equipment in the hex is lost to play
due to being buried in the rubble. If the penetration result is less than
H, no effect. Aperture: Like a wall, except that an H or M result
causes destruction. Door: Any H or M penetration result causes for-
tified building to be destroyed. An L penetration result causes the
door to be breached. If the door is breached, treat that side of the
building like a doorway instead of a window (the door has been blasted
open). A breached door can be crossed by US soldiers to enter the
fortified building. Crew: If the crew is prone, the fire misses. (Ex-
ception: If firing through a breached door, the crew can be hit if
prone.) If the crew is not prone, determine the target hit at random.
Resolve damage against that soldier alone, unless using aimed auto-
matic weapon fire, in which case more than one soldier can be hit.
