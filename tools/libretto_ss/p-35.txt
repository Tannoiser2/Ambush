firing the mortar. US soldiers cannot use
German mortar for any purpose. When a

Damage. If the mortar strikes lands off the
map, no effect. If the mortar strike lands in a
hex with an active target, damage is assessed
as follows. Each soldier occupying a clear,
road, or cover hex rolls on the Grenade Inside
row. If mortar strike hits a building hex, roll
on the Grenade Outside row; if the initial
result is kill, one wall hexside (chosen at
random, place destroyed wall hexside marker)
is destroyed and all soldiers in the hex take
damage on the Semi-Automatic Rifle row; if
the initial result is other than kill, there is no
damage to the building, but all soldiers in the
hex lose 1 Turn (if any remain). If the mortar
strike hits a hex with a vehicle, roll on the
Grenade Outside row. If the initial result is
incapacitated or kill, vehicle is disabled and
vehicle occupants roll on Carbine row; if
initial result is other than incapacitated or kill,
there is no damage to the vehicle, but all
occupants take damage on the Pistol row due
to flying shrapnel. Remember that soldiers in
a hex with a vehicle but not in the vehicle are
considered to be in cover. If any other type of
hex is hit by the mortar strike, each soldier in
the hex takes damage on the Grenade Outside
row.
