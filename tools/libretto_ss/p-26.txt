517. A fortified building hex is a building hex
that the Germans have strengthened with
concrete, steel, and other materials. (If a
fortified building marker is placed in a multi-
hex building, only the hex occupied by the
marker is considered fortified; the rest of the
building is normal. If a fortified building
marker is in a two-story building, only the
first floor is fortified. A collapsing second
story will not damage the fortified first floor.)
A fortified building faces the four sides as
indicated by the fortified building marker and
has a door on one side (doors and windows as
originally indicated on the map have been
blocked up and sealed off). The activation
paragraph will indicate the facing of the door.
All four sides of the fortified building are
considered window apertures for combat and
LOS. Firing at a fortified building, you can
aim at a target visible through an aperture, or
you can fire at the

fortified building itself. To fire at a soldier,
the soldier must be crouching or standing.
However, a soldier crouching in a fortified
building cannot be fired at if your soldier is
adjacent to the fortified building and is also
crouching (see LOS rules in Ambush!) and
vice versa. When firing at the fortified
building, there is a +4 modifier for fire at a
wall. When firing at the fortified building
itself, use the following procedure. First
resolve the fire to determine whether or not
you have hit the fortified building. If you
miss, there is no effect. If you hit, you must
determine which facing you hit; there are only
