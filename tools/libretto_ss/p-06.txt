In Operations, the convoy must stay closed up, with one vehicle per
hex and no gaps, except that there can be a one-hex gap between
the first and second vehicles. Once Rounds are entered, you can move
vehicles as you choose. If you return to Operations, the lead vehicle
must wait where it is until the rest of the active vehicles have once
again closed up the convoy.

Since the backs of the trucks are filled with hot meals for your
company, no one can ride back there. As long as all vehicles are
active, all soldiers must either occupy a seat in the jeep, ride in the
front of the truck, or walk. If a vehicle is inactive, men can hang
on the outside of the trucks (but not the jeep); it costs 1 MP to climb
onto the side of a truck. No more than two men can hang onto one
truck. A non-wounded man can hang on the side of a truck and con-
duct one snap fire per Turn (paying —2 for fire from a vehicle); a
wounded man can only hang on and cannot conduct any fire while
doing so. A man hanging onto the side of a truck is considered a
passenger for purposes of resolving fire combat. If a man hanging
onto the side of a truck is wounded, he falls off and lands prone in
the hex where he was wounded. If a man hanging onto the side of
a truck panics as the result of an IN die roll or combat, he conducts
a PC Check. If he passes, he continues to hang onto the truck and
will not go prone at the end of the Round; if he fails, he falls off
the truck, lands crouching, and falls prone at the end of the Round.

SPECIAL RULES

Jeep. The jeep has an ammo capacity totalling 6 clips. This storage
can be used only to carry MMG ammo (squad members can also
carry ammo for the MMG in their Ammo Boxes). Passenger | fires
the MMG and does not pay the —2 penalty for fire from a vehicle;
any other passenger (not the driver) can assist. If the jeep is disabled,
the MMG is considered destroyed as well. If the jeep is immobi-
lized, the MMG can still be fired, but it cannot be removed from
the jeep.

Trucks. Each truck starts the Mission carrying a load of food for
the rest of your company. If a truck becomes disabled, its load can
be transferred to another active vehicle. The disabled truck and the
vehicle to which the load will be transferred must be in the same
hex. In Operations, it costs 2 Random Event Checks to transfer a
load; in Rounds, it costs 4 Actions (which can be shared) and the
soldier(s) making the transfer must be standing or crouching in:the
hex with the 2 vehicles. After the load has been transferred, make
note on your Squad Record Sheet of which vehicle has the load from
the disabled truck. As long as a truck is still active, its load cannot
be transferred to another vehicle. Any truck can carry up to 3 loads,
but a truck carrying 3 loads can be driven only slowly. The jeep can
carry one load if the only occupant is the driver. The jeep can be
driven only slowly if it is carrying a load of food.

River. The water running from O-1 south is a river and can only
be crossed at the bridge or ford. River hexes are marked XXX on
the Condition Cards to remind you that these hexes cannot be entered.

Cliffs. The cliff hexes are extremely steep and impassable, They can-
not be entered by soldiers or by vehicles. These hexes are also marked
XXX on the Condition Cards to remind you that they cannot be
entered.

German Actions. For this Mission only, if a German is instructed
to throw a grenade at an active American vehicle, he will do so in
the following manner:

1. If in the same hex as the vehicle, the German will attempt to throw
the grenade directly into the vehicle.

2. If in an adjacent hex, the German will attempt to throw the grenade
directly into the vehicle, subject to a —3 modifier for throwing through
a non-adjacent aperture.

3. If at a range of 2 or more hexes, the German will attempt to disable
the vehicle by throwing the grenade into the vehicle’s hex and roll-
ing on the Grenade Outside row. If the result is incapacitated or killed,
the vehicle is disabled and each occupant must roll on the Grenade
Outside row (see page 32 of the Ambush! rules).

VICTORY

There are no VP awards for German activation in this Mission.
Victory Points are awarded as follows: +1 VP for each truck exited;
+2 VP for each load of food exited; +1 VP for each active German
left on the map at the end of the Mission. Bonus VP awards may
also be revealed during play. Victory Points needed, and how the
Mission ends (including exit location) will be revealed during play.
If all 3 trucks are disabled, it will be extremely difficult to win the
Mission. If you have already read the Victory Conditions when the
last truck becomes disabled and feel victory is possible, please try;
if you have not read the Victory Conditions when the last truck
becomes inactive, it is suggested that you start the Mission over.

Mission 21: Surprise,
Surprise!

19 July 1943, Near Gela, Sicily. Following a long and tiring over-
night march, your squad is dozing in the sun while guarding the
approaches to a small bay where supplies are to be delivered later
in the day. This should be easy duty, despite rumors of roving German
units that have not yet been neutralized.

YOUR SQUAD

You can use the pre-generated squad or your own continuing squad,
or you can generate a new squad. If you are campaigning your own
squad, re-roll the Weapon Points and re-arm your squad. In addi-
tion, you receive a radio at no cost.

SET-UP
Use half-map H and full-map F. Place Map H on top of Map F so

that hexes A-1 and Y-1 on Map H are over the same hexes on Map
F. The top of the map is the south edge.

Divide your squad into two 4-man sections. One section is to
set up in the row of Level 3 hexes running from E-15 through I-13
(inclusive); the other section is to set up in any first floor building
hex within 3 hexes of S-14. No more than two men can start in any
one hex.

Place intact stone bridge markers in K-12 (connecting K-13 and
K-11) and T-7 (connecting T-6 and T-8).

SPECIAL RULES

River. The water barrier running from Y-6 to A-12 is a river and
can be crossed only at a bridge or ford. River hexes are marked XXX
on the Condition Cards to remind you that these hexes cannot be
entered.

Cliffs. The cliff hexes are so steep as to be impassable and cannot
be entered by men or vehicles. Cliff hexes are also marked XXX on
the Condition Cards to remind you that these hexes cannot be entered.
