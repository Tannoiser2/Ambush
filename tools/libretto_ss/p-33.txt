730. 1. If there is an active American in the
same hex, assault to kill.

2. If there is an active American in an
adjacent hex and German's WS is +1 or +2,
charge assault to kill.

3. If there is an active American in an
adjacent hex and German’s WS is 0, —1, or
—2, crouch and throw grenade at hex with
most Americans in it and no active German in
same hex. If no grenade is available, conduct
801. If all adjacent hexes that have active
Americans in them also have an active
German in the same hex, charge assault hex
with most Americans in it.

4. If there are 2 or more Germans from this
patrol inactive, move as quickly as possible
away from the nearest active American by
most direct route, going around prohibited
hexes. Fall prone if free stance change is
available and a target is in sight. If out of
sight at the end of the Round and no active
American is within 6 hexes, remove this
German from the map.

5. If an active American is visible, conduct
one snap fire at closest target and then move
as quickly as possible toward nearest active
target by the most direct route, going around
prohibited hexes.

6. If there is no active American in sight,
move as quickly as possible toward nearest
active American by the most direct route,
going around prohibited hexes. Conduct one
snap fire at closest target to come into view if
there are sufficient Movement Points
remaining to do so. Fall prone at end of Turn
if free stance change is available and target is
in sight.

731. In Operations, move through barbed wire
normally. In Rounds, barbed wire costs +1
MP to enter, and each US soldier

must fall prone upon entering the barbed wire
hex. Soldiers can continue to move in the
same Turn if they have sufficient Movement
Points remaining to do so. When a soldier
attempts to leave a barbed wire hex, roll one
die: On a result of 0, the soldier is wounded,
remains prone in the hex, and loses any
remaining Turns in the Round; on each
successive Turn, the soldier must again roll
one die prior to moving to see if he suffers
further damage; on a result of 1-9, there is no
effect. Barbed wire has no effect on German
soldier movement. A hex can be cleared of
barbed wire by successfully setting off a
satchel charge in the hex. If a tracked vehicle
attempts to move through a barbed wire hex,
it costs 2 MP to enter the hex plus an
Accident Check:

* Success: The barbed wire marker is
removed.

* Failure: The vehicle is immobilized in the
hex and the barbed wire remains.

Other vehicles (non-tracked) are prohibited
from barbed wire hexes. Barbed wire counters
have 731 on them to remind you of this
paragraph.
