221. One US soldier (chosen at random, but
not a commander) goes berserk! For the
remainder of the Mission, or until this soldier
or all Germans become inactive, conduct the
following procedure:

1. The soldier receives 2 Turns per Round
(unless wounded).

2. At the start of each Action, the soldier
stands, conducts one snap fire at the closest
target, and then moves toward the closest
active German soldier, using up to half his
MPA (rounded down). The soldier does not
fall prone after movement. If the soldier is
wounded, he will stop firing and move only.
3. If the soldier’s main weapon jams or runs
out of ammo, he will use his secondary
weapon. If no weapons are available, the
soldier will use his full MPA each Turn. The
berserk US soldier will not use grenades.

4. The soldier must move directly toward the
closest active German soldier, paying normal
terrain costs, and rolling one die to resolve
movement choices. The soldier will not enter
prohibited terrain, but will move as quickly as
possible around the obstacles.

5. If the soldier enters a hex or begins a Turn
in a hex containing active German soldiers, he
will assault to kill. If all Germans exit the hex
or become inactive, the berserk soldier will
again move toward the nearest active German
soldier.

6. The soldier is immune to panic, and is
considered to have a +2 WS when attacking
or defending in assault combat.

by successfully tackling him. If any US
