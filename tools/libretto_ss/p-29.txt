588. Patrol boat has 6 Crew Points (not
Tepresented by separate counters). Boat will
spend all available Crew Points to pull squad
members on board unless instructed to fire
first. In such a case, Crew Points remaining
after fire is conducted will be used to pull
squad members on board. Boat will not move
when within 2 hexes of active US soldier.*
When the patrol boat has an IN of 4 or 5, it
exerts a Command Radius of 2 hexes. The
patrol boat’s IN is added to that of all active
US soldiers occupying those hexes for
purposes of determining whether the soldiers
are in command. If the patrol boat's IN is

reduced to 2, it will no longer have a
Command Radius, and the soldiers will
determine command status normally. The
patrol boat itself (and its inherent crew) will
never panic. If patrol boat fails an Accident
Check caused by driving fast, it will stop in
that hex. It can still fire or pull squad
members on board if so directed, but it will
not move further in that Turn. Patrol boat
pays 1 MP per hex entered and follows
movement references on Condition 3 card.
Action costs are as follows: drive slow, 1
Crew Point; drive fast, 2 Crew Points, snap
fire with HMG, 1 Crew Point; aimed fire
with HMG, 2 Crew Points; pull active squad
member on board from adjacent hex, 2 Crew
Points; pull inactive squad member on board
from adjacent hex, 4 Crew Points, and roll
one die with a result of 0 causing an
incapacitated man to be killed and a 1-9
having no effect. Squad members can conduct
fire from boat, using —2 modifier for fire
from vehicle. Grenades thrown from boat
have distance to target doubled for To Hit
determination (grenade thrown at target 2
hexes away is considered to be thrown at
target at 4 hex distance). Grenade cannot be
thrown at target more than 3 hexes from boat.
If boat crew is reduced to 2 or 1, other
passengers can spend Actions to help pull men
on board, with 1 Action being considered 1
Crew Point. Squad members cannot fire the
HMG. If crew is reduced to zero, a squad
member can drive the boat with his DS
reduced by 2. German grenade attacks against
the patrol boat are resolved on the Grenade
Inside row. Boat crew makes 1 PC Check:

* Success: The crew is considered prone.

© Failure: The crew is considered non-prone.
Any result other than panic reduces the crew
by 1. Passengers, if any, get to make the
normal Grenade Strike PC Check. If the
initial result on the damage roll against crew
members is kill, the boat is destroyed an the
entire crew is killed. Any passengers who
survive their damage rolls are placed prone in
an adjacent land hex of your choice. Once all
active US soldiers are on board, the boat crew
will conduct one PC Check per Turn:

© Success: The boat is under your control and
will exit the south edge of the map as fast as
possible.

* Failure: Follow the movement references on
the Condition Card.

Any commander on board can spend an
Action to give the boat crew another PC
Check to attempt to exit, but the crew cannot
be given a Turn for any other purpose. If the
boat is sunk, see 703. Patrol boat counter has
588 on it, referring to this paragraph.
“Excludes naval gun crew.
