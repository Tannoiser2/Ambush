300. As long as the tank is in a building, the
tank can only fire along the row of hexes it is
facing. When the tank is in the hex with the
destroyed wall hexside, the destroyed wall
hexside is considered open for LOS and fire
combat purposes. If a tank is in a building
and the building collapses while the tank is
open, each exposed crew member rolls on the
Pistol row and any result other than panic
reduces the crew by 1. If the tank is closed
and the building collapses on it, make an
Accident Check; if an accident results, a —2
modifier is applied to the roll on the Accident
Table. If a tank is directed to enter a barbed
wire hex, it costs 2 MP to enter the hex plus
an Accident Check. If the Accident Check is
passed, remove the barbed wire counter from
the hex; if the Accident Check fails, the tank
is immobilized in the hex and the barbed wire
remains. Make note of this paragraph on your
Squad Record Sheet.
