674. A guard boat is considered a small
vehicle in the open for fire combat purposes.
Guard boat cannot be entered by Americans
for any purpose. A guard boat has 2 speeds:
slow (1 hex per Turn, for 1 Crew Point); and
fast (2 hexes per Turn, for 2 Crew Points). If
a guard boat is fired on, roll 1 die:

© On an odd result, the crew is hit. If the
result is other than panic, reduce the crew by
1. If the firing weapon is a submachinegun,
keep rolling. If the firing weapon is a
bazooka, the rest of the crew rolls on the
Grenade Inside row, with any result other
than panic reducing the crew by 1 (see
Bazooka rules in Ambush!). If the crew is
reduced to zero, remove the guard boat from
the map; it is considered eliminated for
victory purposes.

* On an even result, the boat is hit. If
penetration is L, the boat can move only
slowly. If penetration is M or H, the boat is
eliminated and removed from the map.
Grenades can be thrown into a guard boat.
There is a —3 modifier applied if the grenade
is thrown from outside the boat’s hex. If the
initial roll causes the grenade to miss, and the
scatter roll causes the grenade to hit, roll 1
die: On an odd result, the grenade lands in
the boat; on an even result, the grenade lands
in the water in the boat’s hex. If a grenade
lands in the guard boat, the guard boat is
destroyed and all crew members are killed;
remove the guard boat from the map. It costs
1 Crew Point to fire bolt rifle and 1 Crew
Point to throw grenade. Guard boat counter
has 674 marked on it to remind you of this
Paragraph.
