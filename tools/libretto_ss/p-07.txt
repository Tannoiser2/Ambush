Mission 22: Another Bridge
Too Far?

January 1944, Northwest of Monte Cassino. Partisans report that there
is a route through the Aurunci Mountains. Headquarters has sent your
squad to see if there are intact bridges over which divisional armor
can move to flank the Gustav Line.

YOUR SQUAD

You can use the pre-generated squad or your own continuing squad,
or you can generate a new squad. If you are campaigning your own
squad, re-roll the Weapon Points number and re-arm your squad.
In addition, at no cost, you receive a half track with a mounted
medium machinegun (the MMG comes with 1 clip; you must buy
any additional ammo for it with your Weapons Points) and a jeep
at no cost. Both vehicles are under your control.

SET-UP

Use full-map F and half-map G. Place Map G on top of Map F so
that hexes A-11 and Y-11 on Map G are over the same hexes on Map
F. The top of the map is the east edge. Your squad enters in the
vehicles through hexes W-1 to Y-1. Place an intact stone bridge
marker in O-4 connecting N-4 and P-3, and in V-14 connecting U-15
and W-14. Place a rubble marker in K-10. No other markers begin
on the map. Activation Levels are as follow: ¢1: 0-3; ¢2: 0-5; ¢3:
0-8; e4: 0-8.

SPECIAL RULES

Stream. The water barrier running from O-1 through N-10 and then
southwest to Y-15 is a stream and can be crossed by spending Move-
ment Points as summarized on the MP Cost Chart.

Cliffs. Cliffs are sloping and can be entered by a soldier at a cost
of 2 MP to enter. Vehicles cannot enter cliff hexes.

Bridges. There are 3 levels to a bridge hex: (1) on top of the bridge;
(2) under the bridge, in the bridge supports (place soldier under the
bridge counter with an inverted prone marker on him); and (3) beneath
the bridge, on the ground (place soldier under the bridge counter
without the inverted prone marker). A soldier under or beneath the
bridge cannot see or be seen by a vehicle or a crouching or prone
soldier on the bridge; LOS exists only between standing soldiers on
the bridge and soldiers under or beneath the bridge. There is LOS
between soldiers under the bridge and those beneath the bridge.
To check a bridge hex, a soldier must be in the hex on top of
the bridge and climb down into the supports (1 Action to climb down
into supports or back up). Due to the nature of the bridge construc-
tion, a soldier cannot climb from the ground beneath a bridge up

into the supports. A soldier in the supports in considered crouching;
a soldier climbing back onto the bridge from the supports has a free
stance change. Since a soldier under a bridge is considered crouch-
ing, it costs 1 Action to move from a hex in the supports to an adjacent
hex in the supports.

A soldier in the supports or beneath a bridge can be seen from
non-bridge hexes as if he were crouching on top of the bridge. Base
Hit modifiers against a target in the supports under a bridge are
determined as follows: when firing at a target from a different hex
at a soldier under a bridge, the target is considered to be crouching
in a hex Interior Through Non-Adjacent Aperture (—4 modifier);
when firing from the top of the bridge at a target in the same hex
under the bridge, the target is considered to be crouching in a hex
Interior Through Adjacent Aperture (—2 modifier). Attacks against
soldiers beneath a bridge on the ground use the modifier for the terrain
and current stance. If a soldier under a bridge is firing at another
soldier under the bridge, the target is considered to be crouching in
the clear (no modifier).

If a soldier panics or is wounded while under a bridge in the
supports, conduct PC Check: If successful, the soldier loses any
remaining Turns this Round, but he does not fall; if he fails, the soldier
falls from his position in the supports under the bridge. If he falls
from under the bridge in O-4 or V-14, he falls into the stream and
is swept away (he is considered KIA and all his equipment and ammo
is lost).

Soldiers under or beneath a bridge can engage in all types of
combat except grenade and satchel charge attacks. Grenades and
satchel charges cannot be thrown from the top of the bridge at targets
under or beneath the bridge, nor can they be thrown from under the
bridge at targets on or beneath the bridge, nor from beneath the bridge
at targets on top of or under the bridge.

Half Track. The half track has ammo capacity totalling 6 clips. This
capacity can be used only to carry MMG ammo. Passenger | fires
the MMG and does not pay the —2 modifier penalty for fire from
a vehicle. Any other passenger (not driver or assistant) can assist
in firing the MMG. If the half track is disabled, the MMG is con-
sidered destroyed as well. If the half track is immobilized, the MMG
can still be fired, but it cannot be removed from the half track.

Embankments. In this Mission, embankment hexsides present no
special hazard to soldiers, but they are too steep for wheeled and
tracked vehicles. Vehicles are prohibited from crossing embankment
hexsides. .

VICTORY

You receive 1 VP for each of the following hexes entered: O-4, W-8,
V-14, K-14, J-14, I-15. Additional Victory Points will be revealed
during play as will the number of Victory Points needed to win and
how the Mission ends.
