748. Germans in patrol are not automatically
aware when placed on the map. Each German
must conduct a PC Check as follows:

* In same hex with active American or mule:
PC (+2).

* Within 3 hexes of visible active American
or mule: PC (+1).

* Otherwise: normal PC.

Any Germans who fail this PC Check are
unaware for the first Round. All Germans in
patrol are automatically aware in the second
and subsequent Rounds.
