8. Random Destruction

Bridges, buildings, walls, sightings, and so forth, which players
encounter during play, should under no circumstances be destroyed
or fired at unless they contain active targets, or their destruction is
specifically allowed in a Mission briefing or in a paragraph instruc-
tion. Players should understand that, if they destroy a bridge without
reason, the game system will not know that the bridge is down, which
will result in illogical movement situations that can ruin a Mission’s
programming.

9. Optional Rules

The following rules can be used entirely at your own option to add
flavor to your Missions. The rules can be used individually or in con-
junction with one another.

9.1 Firing at Targets in Buildings

German soldiers currently have no provision for firing at targets lying
prone in building hexes, because these potential targets are not in

sight and are therefore invalid targets, even though German weapons
are quite capable of penetrating buildings. The following optional
German Action is highly recommended, because it allows the German
soldiers to fire at these hidden targets. Letting them do so is much
more realistic, and it evens things up a bit.

If a German soldier has fired at a visible target in a wooden build-
ing hex, receives a subsequent order to fire again, but has no target
because the US soldier has since fallen prone, the German can now
fire at the wall in an attempt to achieve a penetration result. Conduct
the following procedure for the German:

1. If the paragraph gives no other options for the German (such as
move, surrender, throw a grenade), the German conducts a PC Check.
If the German passes the Check, he will fire at the wall; if he fails,
he will do nothing.

2. If the German is allowed to fire at the wall, he will crouch and
conduct the procedure outlined in 13.5 of the Ambush! rules.
Note that a German tank will conduct the same procedure, but it will
fire its main gun at a stone wall, or its coaxial or bow MG at a wooden
wall.

9.2 Moving an Inactive Soldier

In addition to the procedure for moving an inactive man one hex,
as provided for in the Ambush! rules booklet, the following method
can be used. Two non-wounded soldiers can make a litter using their
rifles by spending 1 Action each, simultaneously. Once the litter is
constructed, the two soldiers can carry the litter a maximum of 3
hexes per Turn, paying normal terrain costs. It costs 1 MP to pick
up or put down the litter, and the rifles used to make the litter cannot
be used in combat while the litter is occupied.

The soldiers carrying the litter cannot engage in any form of fire
combat nor carry any other weapons other than pistols. They can
assault normally after putting the litter down. If assaulted while
carrying the litter, or if one or both litter-bearers panic for any reason,
the litter is dropped and the man on the litter suffers damage on the
Pistol row. When an inactive man is being carried on a litter, it is
not necessary to check for death caused by moving an inactive man.

The two soldiers carrying the litter must be on the same row
of the Action Round Track to spend simultaneous Actions to move
the litter or pick it up or put it down. If one litter bearer has 2 Turns
and the other has only one Turn, they cannot pick up, put down,
or carry the litter until both are on the 1-Turn row (unless, of course,
someone gives the 1-Turn bearer a Turn, raising him to the 2-Turn
row).

Mission 19: All Wet

August 1943. Intelligence reports that the Germans are massing sup-
plies for a counteroffensive at a small cove near Messina. Your squad
is to verify the accuracy of this information and, if confirmed, destroy
as much of the German stockpiles as possible.

YOUR SQUAD

You can use the pre-generated squad or your own continuing squad,
or you can generate a new squad. If you are campaigning your own
squad, re-roll the Weapon Points number and re-arm your squad.
Since you will be travelling light and fast, you can buy weapons from
only the following categories: carbines, submachineguns, pistols,
bazookas, grenades, and clips. In addition and at no cost, you receive
diving equipment (masks, fins, rubber suits, air tanks for 4 squad
members), 4 knives, 4 timed explosives, and 2 canoes (see below
for rules concerning these articles).

SET-UP

Use half-map H and full-map F. Place H on top of F so that hexes
‘A-1 and Y-1 on Map H are over the same hexes on Map F. The top

of the map is the west edge. Your squad enters in hex A-1 with 2
squad members in each canoe and 2 divers hanging on the sides of
each canoe. Divers are considered to be on the surface and must
continue to hang onto the canoes until directed to do otherwise. At
least one commander must be in a canoe. Place intact stone bridge
in T-7 connecting T-6 and T-8; place a ford marker in K-12 connect-
ing K-11 and K-13. The Activation Levels are as follow: ¢1: 0-5;
c2: 0-8.

SPECIAL RULES

Sheet which squad members have knives. A man with a knife receives
a +1 to his WS (for a maximum WS of +2) when making or defend-
ing against an assault. Knives cannot be thrown, nor can any soldier
have more than one knife.
