179. [s8] The barge contains many mules.
There is a crew (not represented in any way)
which will help unload the mules from the
barge into K-8; once the mules are unloaded,
the crew will attend to its other duties, and
neither the crew nor the barge will have any
further part in the Mission. No squad
members can board the barge. At least one
active squad member must be present in K-8
during each attempt to unload a mule. To
unload a mule from the barge into K-8, roll 1
die: On a result of 0-8, the mule is
successfully unloaded (place Mule counter,
Controlled side up, in K-8); on a result of 9,
the mule slips off the gangplank and damage
is determined by rolling 1 die: On a result of
0-2, the mule falls into the bay and drowns,
taking the supplies it carried to the bottom; on
a result of 3-5, the mule falls back into the
barge and is incapacitated; on a result of 6-9,
the mule falls into K-8 and is incapacitated
and unable to carry supplies. If a mule falls
into K-8, each active soldier in the hex
conducts a PC Check. Those that pass jump
clear and are unharmed; those that fail are
struck by the falling mule and roll for damage
on the Pistol row. You lose 1 VP for each
mule that drowns or is incapacitated during
unloading. Once you have successfully
unloaded 6 mules (you cannot take more than
6 with your squad), head for the front lines,
which are reported to be in the vicinity of
Y-10 through Y-14. See 527 for information
about mules. Remember that no more than 4
mules can occupy a hex at one time.
