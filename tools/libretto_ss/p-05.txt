Canoes. Canoes are treated as on page 36 of Ambush! with the
exception that, if a canoe is sunk by fire in a hex not adjacent to
land, the occupants of the canoe have until the end of the current
Round to get the canoe to land. Otherwise, the canoe sinks in its cur-
rent hex (taking with it all the equipment in the canoe Port Boxes)
_and the occupants instantly become swimmers. If a canoe with divers
hanging onto its sides is fired on, the men in the water will let go
of the canoe and conduct their Actions individually as divers and
swimmers rather than simply be carried along with the canoe.
Divers and Swimmers. Divers and swimmers are restricted to using
1 Port Box. Divers are designated by using a marker that indicate
whether they are surfaced or underwater. Any soldier other than a
diver is in an all-water hex is considered a swimmer (as opposed
to a designated diver); place an inverted prone marker on a swimmer
who is currently underwater. Non-wounded swimmers and divers
receive one free stance change per Turn. It costs 2 MP to enter a
new all-water hex when swimming or diving. Swimmers must end
each Round on the surface (to breathe), but they can swim under-
water during the Round. The LOS of a man underwater is restricted
to the same and adjacent hexes.

When firing at a swimmer or a diver, there is a —4 Fire Combat
Modifier applied. A soldier underwater can be seen (and fired upon)
only from the same or an adjacent hex. An American diver or
swimmer in the same hex with a German diver or swimmer must
be in the same stance (underwater or on the surface) to prevent another
German from firing at the American (similarly for a German diver
or swimmer in the same hex with an American); grenade attacks can-
not be conducted against hexes containing both German and American
soldiers. A swimmer or diver can conduct one snap fire per Turn
when on the surface, but he cannot move during a Turn in which
he snap fires (he spends the remainder of his MPA staying afloat
to conduct the snap fire). He can surface prior to firing, or dive
underwater after firing if he has a free stance change available. No
fire combat can be conducted by a man underwater; he can only move,
assault, or stay where he is. Weapons (other than grenades, clips,
knives, and timed explosives, none of which requires protection) can
be waterproofed before the man carrying them dives underwater. It
costs 1 Action to apply or remove waterproofing. If a weapon is taken
underwater without waterproofing, or if a waterproofed weapon is
fired before the protection is removed, the Jam Number becomes
25 (and the Jam Number remains 25 until the soldier spends 1 Action
to clear his weapon, at which time the Jam 25 marker is removed).
Several Jam 25 waterproofing markers are included to help you keep
track; the markers can be placed either on the appropriate soldier

or on the Squad Record Sheet. Note that a bazooka cannot be fired
from an all-water hex.

A swimmer or diver attacked by a grenade makes the normal
grenade strike PC Check (see Ambush!). If he passes, he is considered
prone; if he fails, he is considered non-prone. Grenade attacks against
swimmers and divers are resolved on the Grenade Outside row. If
the grenade strike PC Check is successful and the man who passed
it has at least one Turn remaining in the Round, he can use a Turn
to move to any adjacent hex, making the normal Paragraph Check.
German divers and swimmers who pass the grenade strike PC Check
and have at least one Turn remaining will immediately use a Turn
to move to an adjacent hex, according to the current German move-
ment references. A swimmer or diver on the surface can throw a
grenade up to 3 hexes, applying a —1 modifier due to the unsteady
nature of his condition. A man on the surface can drop a grenade
to attack another man who is underwater; both the man dropping the
grenade and the target make grenade strike PC Checks in this case,
but the man who dropped the grenade adds 2 to his PC for this Check.
A man on the surface cannot use a grenade to attack another man
on the surface in the same hex. A diver or swimmer can only assault
a man who is in the same stance (surface vs. surface, or underwater
vs. underwater).

Combat effects against divers and swimmers are resolved as
follows. Panic: Surface immediately and lose any remaining Turns.
Wounded: Surface immediately, lose any remaining Turns, and drop
1 Port Box of equipment or diving gear. Incapacitated: Surface im-
mediately and move IN marker to Inactive. Killed: Remove counter
from the map (the man sinks to the bottom and all his equipment
is lost).

Diving Equipment. Diving equipment is considered to fill 1 Port
Box, leaving the diver’s other Port Box available to carry a timed
explosive or a weapon. Once the diving equipment is assigned to the
four squad members, it cannot be exchanged with or given to another
squad member, even ifthe man originally assigned the diving equip-
ment is killed or incapacitated.

Night Visibility. To aid in the success of your Mission, it has been
timed for a night with heavy cloud cover. A soldier can see only 3
hexes beyond the hex he occupies.

Cliffs. Cliffs are extremely steep and cannot be entered. Cliff hexes
are marked XXX on the Condition Cards to remind you that they
cannot be entered.

Stream. The water barrier running from A-12 to Y-6 is a stream
and can be entered or crossed by spending Movement Points as
summarized on the MP Cost Chart. Paragraph Checks must be con-
ducted in stream hexes.

Mission 20: Easy as Apple Pie

Christmas Day, 1943, somewhere south of Cassino. Your squad is
just coming off Christmas leave. The first task you are assigned is
to escort a small convoy bringing hot Christmas dinners to the rest
of your company, which has been guarding a strategic tunnel through
the mountains.

YOUR SQUAD

You can use the pregenerated squad or your own continuing squad,
or you can generate a new squad. If campaigning your own squad,
re-roll the Weapon Points number and re-arm your squad. In addi-
tion and at no cost, you receive 3 trucks and a jeep with a mounted
medium machinegun. You must buy ammo for the MMG with your
Weapon Points. Soldiers US 01 and US 02 are members of your squad
for this Mission.

SET-UP

Use Map F. The top of the map is the north edge. All your squad
enters in the vehicles in either A-8 or B-19 (one or the other; not
both). All squad members must stay in the vehicles until a sighting
occurs. Place intact stone bridge marker in O-4 (connecting P-3 and
N-4) and wrecked stone bridge marker in K-12 (connecting K-11 and
K-13). Place wrecked airplane marker (from Ambush/) in K-10. The
Activation Levels are as follow: ¢1: 0-4; €2: 0-6; ¢3: 0-4.

CONVOY PROCEDURES

The jeep and trucks are in convoy and can enter in any order you
choose, using the following procedure:

1. Place the first vehicle in the entry hex and conduct a Paragraph
Check.

2. Advance the first vehicle one hex and conduct a Paragraph Check.
