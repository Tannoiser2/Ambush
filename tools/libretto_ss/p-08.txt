Mission 24: Muleskinner
Blues

October 1943. Near Naples. Your. squad has been sent to a small
village to pick up recently arrived supplies.

YOUR SQUAD

You can use the pre-generated squad or your own continuing squad,
or you can generate a new squad. If campaigning your own squad,
re-roll the Weapon Points number and re-arm your squad.

SET-UP

Use half-maps G and H. The top of the map is the west edge. Place
a ford marker in T-7 connecting T-6 and T-8, and an intact bridge
marker in V-14 connecting U-15 and W-14. There is no 3-hex bridge
in I-15, J-14, K-14 (connecting H-15 and L-13); it has been destroyed
and the wreckage cleared away, and it has not yet been rebuilt. Your
squad sets up with one commander in D-9 and the rest of the squad
in I-9 and J-9. Activation Levels are as follow: ¢1: 0-4; ¢2: 0-6;
3: 0-8.

SPECIAL RULES

Stream. The water barrier running south from Y-6 to the N-column
and then north to Y-15 is a stream and may be crossed by spending
Movement Points as summarized on the MP Cost Chart.

Cliffs. Cliffs are steep and require 1 Action and a Footing Check
to climb up or down a cliff hex.

Bay. The all-water hexes in the southwest corner are a deep bay and
cannot be entered.

VICTORY

Victory Points needed and how the Mission ends will be revealed
during play.

CLIP-FED LIGHT MACHINEGUN

‘This weapon is like the German LMG described in Ambush! section
9.7, with the following exceptions.

4. This weapon takes up only 2 Port Boxes and can be carried by
a single soldier without having to be broken down.

2. The weapon can be fired by a standing or crouching soldier.
3. It uses clips of ammo, which are limited in number (as shown by
the number of available clips listed on the Assistant Machinegun-
ner’s soldier card). Each time the clip-fed LMG is fired, ammo deple-
tion must be rolled for using the out of ammo number of 50. Clip-fed
LMG and clips must be in the same hex to fire the weapon.

Firing the Clip-Fed LMG

4. If the machinegunner is directed to fire the weapon, he will do
so only if clips are available in the hex. If directed to fire and no
clips are available in the hex, but the assistant is active, the
machinegunner will fire his personal weapon instead. If directed to
fire the weapon and no clips are available in the hex and the assistant
with clips is inactive within 4 MPs of the machinegunner’s hex, the
machinegunner will ignore his Action paragraphs and move as quickly
as possible to the hex where the inactive assistant with clips is, crouch,
and pick up all available clips (1 Action). He will then return to where
the clip-fed LMG is. Once the machinegunner has returned with the
clips to the hex with the weapon, he will again conduct normal Ac-
tion paragraphs. If the machinegunner is directed to fire the weapon
and there are no clips in the hex and no clips available within 4 MPs,
he will use his personal weapon when directed to conduct fire combat.

2. If the assistant is directed to fire or assist and is not in the hex
with the clip-fed LMG or is in the hex but no clips are available,
the assistant will fire his personal weapon instead. If the assistant
is directed to fire or assist and is in the hex with the weapon and
clips are available, he will do as directed.

3. If the assistant is active and the machinegunner is wounded or in-
active, on Turns where the assistant starts in a hex without the weapon,
he will stop moving if the hex with the weapon is entered and will
fall prone if free stance change is available or he has sufficient MPs
remaining. On the next Turn, the assistant will crouch and pick up
the weapon (1 Action).

Americans can use the clip-fed LMG with —1 applied to the firing
soldier's WS. There must be clips available in the same hex for the
weapon to be fired. Clips take up 1 Ammo Box per clip. Clip-fed
LMG uses the LMG row of the Weapon Type Chart.
Abandoning the Clip-Fed LMG

‘The German carrying the weapon will immediately abandon it if no
clips are available (all have been used), or clips are available but are
more than 4 MPs from the soldier carrying the weapon and the sold-
ier with the clips is inactive, or the soldier carrying the weapon be-
comes wounded.
